PROJECTMANAGER AI AFRICA — LOCAL HTML EDITION

1. Extract this ZIP (in Windows, right-click it and choose Extract All).
2. Open projectmanager-ai-africa.html with Google Chrome.
3. Click Templates at the top of the application.
4. Choose a project, or leave it as a blank template.
5. Click Use template, enter your information, then Generate template.
6. Edit the generated content, then save it to project documents or download it.

The library contains 16 project management templates. It needs no internet,
installation, online account or AI key. These are structured templates populated
from your inputs and saved records, not live AI-generated assessments.

If you used an older file, open that file first and click Export backup on the
home screen. In this edition use Restore backup to bring your projects over.
Browser storage may depend on the file location. Keep regular JSON backups.

Local records are stored only in this browser profile. There is no login-based
security or online synchronisation. Use the full server app for protected team
workspaces. HTML and text exports are available; Chrome Print can save as PDF.

KNOWLEDGE CENTRE
Click Knowledge Centre in the header for 538 project and operations management
definitions across 21 topics. Search terms or acronyms, filter by topic or A-Z,
open related terms, save bookmarks and export the filtered glossary as CSV.
Selected terms include formulas, conditions and illustrative examples.
The definitions work offline; external further-reading links need internet.
Bookmarks stay in this browser. Summaries are an educational reference; use your
organisation's required definitions for formal decisions.

LEARN & REFRESH
The Knowledge Centre now opens with 24 practical lessons, six learning paths and
48 scenario questions. Each lesson includes a worked decision, common mistakes,
an application task and further reading. Passing a practice check schedules
reviews after 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days. Your progress stays in this browser.
Use Practice lab for earned value, PERT, flow and equipment-effectiveness
exercises. Current guidance contains dated official-source summaries; it is a
curated snapshot, not a live feed. Click Glossary for the searchable definitions.
Lessons and calculations work offline; external reading requires internet.
Practice passes do not award certification, training hours or PDUs.
